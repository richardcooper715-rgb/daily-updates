      Data notes. Prices are closing prices for 16 Oct 2026 (US) and 19 Oct 2026 (UK). Latest-period results, balance sheets, broker views and news come from company releases, SEC/RNS filings and market coverage gathered on 20 Oct 2026; each item links to its source. Earlier fiscal years are compiled from company annual reports. Figures marked ≈ are estimates or derived values, so check them against the filing before relying on them. Broker consensus figures come from aggregators (MarketBeat, Benzinga, S&P Global via StockAnalysis, Bullstory and others) whose analyst pools and dates differ.

      Method. EV = market cap + gross debt − cash and investments (latest balance sheet, leases excluded unless stated). ROA, ROE and ROIC use average opening/closing balances; ROIC = EBIT × (1 − 25%) ÷ (debt + equity). The 3-year and 5-year returns are averages of the annual ratios. CAPE = price ÷ average inflation-adjusted EPS over the last 10 fiscal years (US CPI-U for USD reporters, UK CPI for sterling). Dividend growth is the compound annual growth in declared dividend per share. For HSBC, enterprise value, EBITDA and debt ratios are not meaningful, so bank metrics are shown instead.
